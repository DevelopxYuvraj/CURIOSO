CULTURAL DISCOVERY

From Gyan Chaupar to digital play

Indian board-game traditions include historical forms of Snakes and Ladders known as Gyan Chaupar. Museum collections preserve Indian examples, including a 19th-century Pahari board.

CURIOS digital version uses a modern 100-square ruleset for accessible gameplay.
