How to Play

🤖

Quick Tips

  • Use controlled shots.
  • Avoid wasting your turns.
  • Target groups of marbles.
  • Hard mode has a stronger computer.
CULTURAL DISCOVERY

Kancha — A Traditional Marble Game

Kancha, also known as goli in different regions, refers to traditional marble games played by children in many parts of India. Players use small glass or stone marbles and develop aiming, timing and hand-eye coordination through play.

🟤

Simple Materials

Traditional play commonly uses small colourful marbles that are easy to carry and share.

🎯

Aiming Skill

Players practise accuracy, timing and controlled movement while aiming at targets.

👥

Shared Play

Marble games have been played socially among children in many communities.

Digital adaptation: This game is a simplified digital interpretation inspired by traditional Indian marble games.
