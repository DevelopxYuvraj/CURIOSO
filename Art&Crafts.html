Madhubani Art

Madhubani painting is a traditional folk art associated with Bihar. It is known for detailed patterns, bright colours and themes inspired by nature, mythology and everyday life.

📍 Origin

Bihar

🎨 Style

Folk Painting

🧰 Materials

Natural colours & paper

🌿 Theme
